Simple Process

From Pitch Deck to Funding

A streamlined 4-step process designed to get you funded faster without the bureaucracy.

01

Submit Pitch Deck

Upload your one-pager or pitch deck. Tell us about your product, traction, and ask.

02

Get Shortlisted

Our experts review your startup. If selected, you get on the Karo Pitch roster.

03

Pitch Live

Present your vision in our curated virtual or physical demo days for investors.

04

Raise & Scale

Negotiate term sheets and secure the funding needed to take your business to the next level.
